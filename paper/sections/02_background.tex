\section{Background}
\label{sec:background}

\subsection{Group-relative policy optimisation}
\label{sec:grpo}

\draft{Standard GRPO/RLOO setup. Define prompt $x$, group of $G$ completions
$y_1,\dots,y_G$, verifiable reward $r_i$, advantages.}

GRPO \citep{shao2024grpo} samples a group of $G$ completions per prompt and
normalises rewards within the group,
\begin{equation}
  \hat A_i = \frac{r_i - \operatorname{mean}(r_{1:G})}{\operatorname{std}(r_{1:G})},
\end{equation}
while RLOO \citep{ahmadian2024rloo} uses the leave-one-out mean as baseline,
$\hat A_i = r_i - \tfrac{1}{G-1}\sum_{j\neq i} r_j$. Both optimise the clipped
surrogate
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}(\theta) = -\,\mathbb{E}\Big[\min\big(\rho_{i,t}\hat A_i,\;
    \operatorname{clip}(\rho_{i,t},1-\epsilon,1+\epsilon)\hat A_i\big)\Big],
  \label{eq:surrogate}
\end{equation}
with the per-token ratio
$\rho_{i,t} = \pi_\theta(y_{i,t}\mid x, y_{i,<t}) / \pi_{\text{old}}(y_{i,t}\mid x, y_{i,<t})$.
In the single-epoch setting $\pi_{\text{old}}=\pi_\theta$ at sampling time,
so $\rho_{i,t}=1$ in the forward pass and the ratio contributes only through
its gradient. \draft{Note: in our loop the sampling-pass log-probs are reused
as $\log\pi_{\text{old}}$, so there is no separate scoring forward.}

\subsection{KV eviction and the sparse-rollout mismatch}
\label{sec:eviction}

Heavy-hitter eviction \citep{zhang2023h2o} and sink-plus-window schemes
\citep{xiao2024streamingllm} bound the KV cache at $K$ slots and drop entries
by an accumulated-attention score. When the \emph{sampler} is sparse but the
\emph{trained policy} is dense, the samples are drawn from
$\pi^{\text{sparse}}$ while \cref{eq:surrogate} is evaluated under
$\pi^{\text{dense}}$; Sparrow \citep{sadhukhan2026sparrow} shows training
collapses once the lower tail of the per-token acceptance rate
$\min(\pi^{\text{dense}}/\pi^{\text{sparse}},1)$ falls below roughly $0.86$.
\draft{Position this: our design sidesteps the mismatch by keeping sampler
and policy identical, at the price of training a sparse policy.}
