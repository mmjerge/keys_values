\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Sparse rollout for RL.}
Sparrow \citep{sadhukhan2026sparrow} accelerates the rollout of RLVR with
block-sparse attention (Quest-style page selection \citep{tang2024quest}) and
trains a dense policy on the sparse samples, showing that stability is
governed by the lower tail of the per-token acceptance rate and proposing a
dynamic sparsity schedule plus LoRA distillation (DistillSparse) to push
sparsity further. It targets long generation (37k-token math CoT on Polaris
\citep{an2025polaris}, coding on TACO \citep{li2023taco}) with Qwen3 thinking
models from 1.7B to 14B on multi-node H200 clusters, and reports rollout
speedup of 2.0--2.4$\times$. We use the same benchmark family (Polaris and
TACO for training; AIME, AMC, MATH500, LiveCodeBench, HumanEval+, MBPP+ for
evaluation) and the same model family. Our setting differs on two axes: the
sparsity is in the gradient pass as well as the rollout, so there is no
actor--policy mismatch; and the resource constraint is single-GPU memory
rather than cluster throughput. \draft{How to compare head to head is an open
decision; see \cref{app:sparrow}.}
\draft{Also cite any other sparse-rollout / staleness-correction work Sparrow
compares against: TIS \citep{espeholt2018impala}, and the mismatch-correction
papers (Liu et al.\ 2025b/c, Luo et al.\ 2026) once we have the exact refs.}

\paragraph{KV cache compression.}
H2O \citep{zhang2023h2o}, StreamingLLM \citep{xiao2024streamingllm}, SnapKV
\citep{li2024snapkv} and Quest \citep{tang2024quest} bound the cache at
inference time by eviction or page selection; KIVI \citep{liu2024kivi}
quantises it. These are inference-time methods; we use eviction and 8-bit
quantisation inside the training step and measure what the policy can learn
to do with a bounded cache.

\paragraph{Memory-efficient RL for LLMs.}
GRPO \citep{shao2024grpo} and RLOO \citep{ahmadian2024rloo} remove the critic;
DAPO \citep{yu2025dapo} and DeepScaleR \citep{luo2025deepscaler} tune the
recipe for long CoT. Systems such as verl \citep{sheng2025verl} and TRL
\citep{vonwerra2020trl} address throughput across many GPUs. Our contribution
is orthogonal: the per-step memory of a single device.

\paragraph{RLVR benchmarks.}
Competition math (AIME, AMC, MATH500 \citep{lightman2023letsverify}) and
execution-checked code (LiveCodeBench \citep{jain2024livecodebench},
HumanEval+/MBPP+ \citep{liu2023evalplus}) are the standard RLVR evaluation
suite \citep{guo2025deepseekr1,yu2025dapo,luo2025deepscaler}. Long-input
benchmarks such as HELMET \citep{yen2025helmet} and RULER
\citep{hsieh2024ruler} probe the complementary long-prompt regime; our
earlier HELMET results appear in \cref{app:helmet}.
